Routines Guide

💡

What are Workout Routines?

Workout routines are pre-planned workout templates that save you time at the gym! Instead of manually entering exercises every workout, create a routine once and load it whenever you need it. Think of it as your personal workout blueprint.

➕

How to Create a Routine

  1. 1 Click "Create New Routine" - The blue button at the top
  2. 2 Set a date - This will be used when you execute the routine (usually today)
  3. 3 Name your routine - e.g., "Push Day", "Leg Day", "Full Body"
  4. 4 Add a description - Optional, but helpful to remember the routine's purpose
  5. 5 Click "Add Exercise" - Add each exercise with body part, sets, reps, and weight
  6. 6 Click "Save Routine" - Done! Your routine is now saved

💡 Pro Tip: You can also load an existing routine as a starting point and modify it!

🏋️

How to Use Your Routines

In the Workout Logger:

  1. • Go to "Log Workout" page
  2. • Click "Load Routine" in the sidebar
  3. • Select your routine from the dropdown
  4. • Check off exercises as you complete them
  5. • Adjust weights/reps as needed (won't change the saved routine)

✅ Important: Changes you make while logging a workout won't affect your saved routine. It's just a template!

✏️

Editing & Managing Routines

To Edit a Routine:

  • • Click on any routine card to expand it
  • • Click the "Edit" button (pencil icon)
  • • Make your changes
  • • Click "Save" to update

To Delete a Routine:

  • • Expand the routine card
  • • Click the "Delete" button (trash icon)
  • • Confirm deletion

To Create a Copy:

  • • Use "Load Existing Routine" dropdown
  • • Choose "Create NEW routine" when prompted
  • • Modify and save with a new name
👥

Sharing Routines with Friends

Make Your Routines Public:

  1. 1. When creating or editing a routine, toggle "Make Public"
  2. 2. Public routines are visible to your friends
  3. 3. Friends can copy your public routines to their account

Copy Routines from Friends:

  1. 1. Go to the "Friends" page from the main menu
  2. 2. View a friend's profile
  3. 3. Click "View Routines" to see their public routines
  4. 4. Click "Copy to My Routines" to import their routine

🔒 Privacy: Private routines are only visible to you. Make routines public if you want friends to see and copy them!

⚡

Quick Tips

✨ Organize by workout split: Create routines for Push, Pull, Legs, Upper Body, etc.

🎯 Include both strength and cardio: Mix and match exercise types in one routine

📊 Track your progress: Use the same routine regularly to see your improvements

🔄 Update as you progress: Edit routines when you increase weights or change exercises

👥 Learn from friends: Copy public routines from your fitness community

🔒 Control your privacy: Make routines public to share or keep them private for yourself
